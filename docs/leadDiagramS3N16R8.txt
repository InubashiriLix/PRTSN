Notes:
  Module: ESP32-S3-N16R8 / ESP32-S3-WROOM-1, 16MB flash, 8MB OPI PSRAM.
  Arduino FQBN: esp32:esp32:esp32s3.
  BOOT button pulls GPIO00 to GND.
  RST button pulls CHIP_PU / EN to GND.
  Onboard RGB LED is WS2812B-compatible on GPIO48.
  Native USB uses GPIO19 USB_D- and GPIO20 USB_D+.
  UART bridge uses U0TXD and U0RXD.
